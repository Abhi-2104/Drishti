# News whitelist — polled on schedule, scoped to watchlist. NOT open web search.
# Weight Tier 1 > Tier 2. Never add tip blogs / pump Telegram feeds.

## Tier 1 — Primary (highest weight)
- NSE announcements + RSS: https://www.nseindia.com/static/rss-feed
- BSE corporate announcements
- SEBI: https://www.sebi.gov.in
- Ratings: CRISIL / ICRA / CARE / India Ratings (often LEAD price)

## Tier 2 — Reputable media (context)
- Moneycontrol, Economic Times–Markets, Business Standard, LiveMint
- Investing.com India (RSS + econ-events calendar)
- Reuters India / Bloomberg (global-macro)

## Tier 3 — management
- Per-stock keyword Google Alerts RSS (one per watchlist name)
- Organise with Feedly / Inoreader / rss.app

## Excluded
- Stock-tip blogs, pump Telegram channels. Noise at best, manipulation at worst.

## Rules
- Tier-1 filing beats Tier-2 take on conflict. Never upgrade conviction on media alone.
