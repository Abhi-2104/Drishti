   - Contraction: FII selling + midcaps lagging + defensive rotation. Favor defensives, cash.
   - Expansion (mid): midcaps outperforming + FII buying. Favor cyclicals, midcaps, capex.
   - Recovery (early): rates bottoming + DII support + defensives cooling. Favor rate-sensitives,
     financials, early cyclicals.

   - Q3 Jul–Sep: post-budget rebalancing + festive pre-run.
   - Q4 Oct–Dec: festive consumer surge + FII tax-selling risk.
5. BOTTOM LINE — risk-on / neutral / risk-off + the 2-3 sectors to lean into + the one thing
