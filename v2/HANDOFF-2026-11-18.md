## 6. Decision layer, scoring and test method

- **Scorer.** Stock return vs Nifty 50 over 60 sessions, cost 0.4%, WAIT = 0. `compare` = paired date-block bootstrap.
- **Overlap warning.** Cases are 25 sessions apart but score over 60, so they overlap; only ~38 non-overlapping 60-session
  windows exist in 9 years. **Blind tests have low power; do not over-read one number.**
- **Baselines.** `rules-v1` (frozen; reads `price_context`, `insider_cluster`, `pledge`, `macro_regime`; does NOT read
  `deal_flow` / `delivery_trend`) and always-long.
- **History test.** `decisions history` (every=10, n_stocks=10, seed 11, liquid pool = top 300 by 30-day NSE turnover,
  `min_sessions_before=260`), then `history-report` and `history-breakdown` (by action / trend / strength / macro /
  chasing / midcap / uptrend-share / hindsight breadth, halves 2017–21 and 2022–26).
- **Blind tests.** `decisions cases | rules | export | import | compare`. **Always `--gate entry`** (forgetting it creates
  90 macro cases). `--masked` hides dates (offsets), rescales prices (latest close = 100), drops market levels and month
  names; masked is for **stock gates only**. The key file stays on the laptop. Dated runs score higher than masked
  (hindsight contamination).

## 7. Results so far (the table to trust)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| rules-v1 vs always-long, 10-y history | **tie** |
| Breakdown: ENTER picks vs average stock | +2.69% vs +1.18% (both halves), but rules sit out 73% of cases; WAIT/AVOID cases beat Nifty +1.63 / +2.63 in 2022–26 |

| Macro-hostile AVOID | −2.75 per case (second half only); "chasing" rule −1.49 |
