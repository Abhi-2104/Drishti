Judge whether to keep: $ARGUMENTS.
Start with ONE plan line before any tool call: what you are about to pull and why, in plain words at the active level (the first sentence the user sees).
1. get_thesis for this stock first: what I wrote, entry, stop, target, what must stay true, exit triggers and past re-checks. No thesis on file: say so, use my earlier calls (get_decisions), and offer to write one.
2. gather_evidence with gate='exit', query='$ARGUMENTS', as_of = the latest stored session, blind=false.
3. CONTEXT, NOT SCORED: the logged call cites only signals from the evidence bundle (that keeps the scored test clean). Everything else below is context you may reason from and show in the tables, marked 'context' in the Source column, never in the signals list: get_fundamentals (quality floor: ROCE and ROE above about 15%, debt-to-equity below about 1.0, operating cash flow tracking profit; banks and NBFCs by ROE, price-to-book and growth instead; valuation against the stock's own history and peers where the data allows), get_deals (bulk and block), get_announcements and get_credit_ratings (last 30 days), get_calendar (the next results date). Fundamentals have no point-in-time history: say so, and never let them alone decide a call. Also get_trade_levels to compare the current close with my stop.
4. Test the three exit triggers in a table (Trigger | Test | Reading | Source | Fired?): THESIS BREAK (rating downgrade, rising pledge, insider-selling cluster, guidance cut, margin or order-book deterioration, or any 'must stay true' line that no longer holds), TARGET HIT, STOP HIT (close at or below my stop). Price alone is never a thesis break. Market regime and smart-money positioning are context that raises the odds a break is coming, not a trigger.
5. Verdict HOLD, TRIM or EXIT, naming the trigger that fired with its source and date, or "no trigger, HOLD because ...". Rules for the logged call as in /drishti-entry (bundle only, observable invalidation, confidence 0.50-0.99, record_decision with agent 'claude-code').
6. recheck_thesis (intact or not, and what changed); close=true only if I say I have sold or the thesis broke. Propose an updated thesis line if it evolved.
7. Close with the SEBI line.

Format: follow the Drishti answer shape (and the active level, if one is loaded; otherwise use the investor level). Headline (**VERDICT** · subject · horizon vs yardstick · logged id or "not logged" · confidence), the read, then an evidence table (Signal | Reading | Against what | Visual | As of | Source | Says; 🟢 for, 🔴 against, 🟡 mixed, ⚪ unknown; unknowns as rows), with text visuals (bars █░, range gauge ├──●──┤, sparklines ▁▃▇, ▲▼ deltas) where they add information; for calls a scenario table (Case | Trigger | What the data would show | Call becomes), a track-record table when the log has related calls, a blind-spots table (Missing | Why it matters | How to fill it), and a final "Sources:" line naming each provider (NSE bhavcopy, NSE participant OI, NSE insider-trading disclosures, NSE shareholding filings, FRED, Yahoo Finance, MoSPI, CFTC, BSE, NSDL) with its as-of date. Every figure needs its date, its source and a yardstick; differences are computed. Compare = a column per stock; brief = Area | Reading | Against what | Visual | Source | Lean; review = Call | Date | Outcome | vs yardstick.
